Ask these before paying a token

Six questions that settle most disputes later. Get the answers inside the written agreement, not on a call.

Deposit refund

Exact refund timeline (days after vacating) and the full list of allowed deductions, with painting charges capped in writing.

Water & maintenance

Is water Cauvery, borewell or tanker? What does maintenance cover, and what is charged separately (water tanker, clubhouse, generator)?

Lock-in & notice

Lock-in period, notice period, and what happens to the deposit if you leave inside the lock-in.

Owner identity

Owner's name and ID, matching the person (or registered power-of-attorney holder) signing the agreement.

Every charge in writing

Rent, deposit, brokerage, one-time charges, maintenance and escalation percentage — all inside the agreement, all against receipts.

Society rules

Move-in charges, move-in day/time rules, pet and guest policies, and whether the society levies a non-refundable fee on new tenants.

How the flags work

Thresholds, not verdicts. Edit the numbers to match your quote and use the flags as negotiation points.

Deposit

Bengaluru landlords typically ask 5–10 months vs 1–3 in most Indian cities; 6 and under is a reasonable target. This tool does not assess local legal limits on deposits. Treat the number as fully negotiable and get the refund terms in writing.

Brokerage

One month's rent is the common full-service rate; more than one month is a red flag. Zero-brokerage listings exist — factor the broker fee into the flat's true cost either way.

Tanker water

Fully or mostly tanker-dependent buildings can add roughly ₹1,500–3,000/month in summer and mean rationing risk. Whitefield, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road and Marathahalli are the usual suspects — ask the society, not the broker.
